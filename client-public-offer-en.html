Public offer for clients

The taxi service

Version of 25 November 2026

1. General provisions

This public offer is a proposal by The taxi service (the Service) to enter into an agreement on the use of the mobile app for ordering a taxi.

Installing the app, signing in with a phone number and placing an order mean full and unconditional acceptance of these terms.

2. Subject of the agreement

The Service accepts the client's order, passes it to a partner driver and tells the client the car and driver details. The ride itself is performed by the driver.

3. Orders and cancellation

The client sets the pickup address, the fare class and, if needed, extra services. An order can be cancelled before the car arrives.

If a client cancels often, the Service may temporarily limit orders from that client.

4. Price and payment

The ride price is calculated using the fares shown in the app. The final amount is shown by the taximeter at the end of the ride.

Payment is made to the driver in cash or with bonuses where the Service's rules allow it.

5. Bonuses

Bonuses are earned and spent under the Service's rules. Bonuses cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to other people.

6. Client's obligations

Give correct details and address, treat the car with care, behave properly in the car and wear a seat belt.

7. Rating the driver

After the ride the client may rate the driver with stars. The rating counts towards the driver's score.

8. Liability

The Service is not liable for delays caused by traffic, weather, connection failures or other circumstances beyond its control.

9. Changes and contacts

The Service may change these terms; the new version is published at this same link. Continuing to use the app means you accept it.

Questions and complaints are accepted through support in the app menu.
