Privacy policy for drivers

Version of 25 November 2026

1. What data we collect

Full name, phone number, call sign, driving document details, car details (model, colour, plate number), location while working, order history, payments, rating and device details.

2. Why we need it

To distribute orders, show the car and driver to the client, keep accounts, keep rides safe and improve the Service.

3. Location

While the driver is on duty, the app sends the driver's location, including when the app is minimised: this is needed to distribute orders and show the car to the client. Off duty, location is not collected.

4. Who we share it with

With the client during the order: name, car details, a contact number and the car's location. With the Service's dispatchers. With public authorities only where the law requires it. We do not sell data to third parties.

5. Storage and protection

Data is stored on the Service's servers while the driver works with the Service and for the periods set by law. Access to it is restricted and protected.

6. Your rights

You can find out what data we keep about you, correct it or stop working with the Service by contacting the dispatch office.

7. Changes

A new version of this policy is published at this same link. Continuing to work in the app means you accept it.
