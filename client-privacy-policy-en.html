Privacy policy for clients

Version of 25 November 2026

1. What data we collect

Phone number, name, location (while the app is open), addresses and order history, ratings given to drivers, and device details: model and app version.

2. Why we need it

To accept and complete orders, show nearby cars, connect the client with the driver, award bonuses, keep rides safe and improve the Service.

3. Who we share it with

With the driver during the order: name, phone number for contact and pickup address. With public authorities only where the law requires it. We do not sell data to third parties.

4. Location

Location is used only while the app is open: for the pickup address and to show nearby cars. You can turn access off in the phone settings.

5. Storage and protection

Data is stored on the Service's servers while the account is active and for the periods set by law. Access to it is restricted and protected.

6. Your rights

You can find out what data we keep about you, correct it or delete your account by contacting support in the app menu.

7. Changes

A new version of this policy is published at this same link. Continuing to use the app means you accept it.
