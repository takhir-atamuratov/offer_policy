Public offer for drivers

The taxi service

Version of 25 November 2026

1. General provisions

This public offer is a proposal by The taxi service (the Service) to a partner driver to use the driver app to receive orders.

Registering in the app and going on duty mean full and unconditional acceptance of these terms.

2. Subject of the agreement

The Service gives the driver access to clients' orders. The driver performs the rides personally and is responsible for the car, documents and permits.

3. Driver requirements

A valid driving licence, a sound and clean car that matches the chosen fare class, and polite treatment of clients.

4. Orders

Orders are distributed by queue, distance and rating. Accepting orders, arriving on time and cancellations affect the driver's rating.

5. Payment for the Service's services

The driver pays for the Service's services at the current rates from the balance in the app. If the balance is too low, access to orders may be limited.

6. Rating

The rating is formed under the Service's rules, including client ratings, and affects the order in which orders are received.

7. Liability and blocking

The driver is responsible for following traffic rules, passenger safety and damage caused through their fault. For violations the Service may temporarily or permanently close access to the app.

8. Changes and contacts

The Service may change these terms; the new version is published at this same link. Continuing to work in the app means you accept it.

Questions are handled by the Service's dispatch office.
